Take Your Seat at the Table

Buy, build and trade your way across Black America — from Hale's Ford to Harlem, from Greenwood to the National Mall. The board has three lanes, one for each of Washington, Garvey and Du Bois. Every turn you roll three dice: the lane dice picks whose road you walk, and the two number dice say how far. Every time you pass Community Advancement, you decide whether to lift your people up — and your community remembers.

You don't pick one. The lane dice decides each turn, and your lane's rules, deck and Confession apply until your next roll.

📱↻ Turn your phone sideways for a bigger board — the game keeps going when you rotate.
